# Therapy identity and supplied restrictions

`resolve_therapy_identity` is the public identity API for single agents,
regimens, alternative-agent labels and treatment classes. Its result retains
the supplied name, canonical display, comparable key, explicit components and
whether the name is registered. Unrecognized names remain visibly unregistered;
there is no fuzzy or substring-based drug matching.

The existing therapeutic-agent registry now also includes clinical names without
an RNA target annotation. These names participate in history and evidence joins
without becoming new expression-based target nominations. `identity_kind`
distinguishes an agent, regimen, alternatives, class and unspecified product. `components` records
regimen constituents; `identity_source` and `identity_reviewed_at` document name
curation. Approved-brand mappings are sourced to the NCI drug index and linked
individual drug pages. Development/display aliases also retain their curated
panel provenance.

A supplied contraindication to a component excludes a regimen containing it.
Progression, lack of benefit, intolerance and current treatment remain scoped to
the named treatment; they do not automatically exclude a different combination.
A regimen-level event with no identified causative component does not become a
restriction on every component individually.

Products that require separate formulation identity retain separate canonical
names. The registry's `parent_agent` records the underlying drug for those
products. A generic contraindication applies to a product containing that drug.
A formulation-specific contraindication prevents shortlisting a row explicitly
registered as an unspecified product until that ambiguity is reconciled; it does not equate distinct specified
products or assert cross-formulation toxicity.

Named historical treatments remain in the detailed audit even when their
contraindication also blocks a combination. The structured history retains the
original name, normalized identity, component keys and source/file provenance.
The report's history paragraph is generated by the shared named-template path.

This honors restrictions explicitly supplied by the user. It is not an exhaustive
contraindications, interaction or clinical fitness database. An unrestricted
shortlist entry does not establish medical safety.
